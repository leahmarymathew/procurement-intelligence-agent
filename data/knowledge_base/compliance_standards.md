# Compliance Standards and Certification Requirements

## Mandatory Certifications by Category

All suppliers must maintain current certifications appropriate to their category. Lapsed certifications trigger automatic watch-list placement pending renewal confirmation.

MRO and Industrial Supplies: ISO 9001:2015 (Quality Management), OHSAS 18001 or ISO 45001 (Occupational Health & Safety). Suppliers without ISO 9001 may operate on conditional status for orders under $25,000 pending audit.

IT Hardware and Electronics: ISO 9001:2015, ISO/IEC 27001 (Information Security) for any supplier handling data-bearing assets. Export-control compliance (SCOMET classification awareness) is mandatory; failure to maintain classification records is a moderate compliance finding.

Chemical and Hazardous Materials: ISO 14001 (Environmental Management), Responsible Care certification, valid MSDS documentation for all product lines. Non-compliance results in immediate disqualification.

IT Services and Software: ISO/IEC 27001, SOC 2 Type II (for cloud-hosted services), GDPR/PDPA data handling acknowledgment where applicable.

Raw Materials and Mechanical Components: ISO 9001:2015, Material Test Reports (MTR) for all structural materials. Conflict minerals declaration (Dodd-Frank Section 1502 aligned) required annually.

## Audit Outcomes Classification

Audit findings are classified as: Minor (documentation gaps, process deviations with no material impact), Moderate (compliance gap with potential material impact, remediation required within 90 days), Major (active non-compliance, operations suspended pending corrective action), Critical (regulatory violation, immediate disqualification).

Two or more Moderate findings within a 12-month period automatically escalates to Major classification for review purposes.

## Procurement Policy Alignment

All active suppliers must have signed the current Supplier Code of Conduct (v3.2, effective January 2024). Suppliers onboarded before 2024 must have re-signed by April 2024. Unsigned CoC status = compliance score ceiling of 6/10.

Anti-bribery and corruption (ABC) declaration required annually. Failure to submit within 30 days of the renewal deadline results in automatic conditional status placement.

## Geographic and Concentration Risk Thresholds

Single-location suppliers are flagged for geographic risk if the location is in a region with a risk index above 3.5 (internal scale). Dependency concentration risk is triggered when a supplier sources more than 40% of critical inputs from a single tier-2 vendor.

Cross-border procurement from sanctioned jurisdictions is prohibited and triggers immediate disqualification regardless of other scores.
