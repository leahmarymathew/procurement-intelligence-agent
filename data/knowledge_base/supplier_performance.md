# Supplier Performance Records

## Acme Industrial Supplies — Performance Review 2024

Acme Industrial Supplies maintained a 97.3% on-time delivery rate across 412 orders in FY2024. Average lead time was 4.2 days against a contracted SLA of 5 days. Two minor SLA breaches were recorded in Q3 due to logistics partner disruptions; both were resolved within 48 hours with compensatory credit issued.

Category coverage: MRO, Safety Equipment, Industrial Fasteners. Acme holds preferred-supplier status in the MRO category as of January 2025. No quality rejections recorded in the last 18 months. Customer satisfaction score: 4.6/5 across 38 procurement team ratings.

Financial stability: Acme reported $142M revenue in FY2024 with 6.2% YoY growth. Debt-to-equity ratio of 0.38. No reported liens or adverse financial events. Credit rating: BBB+ (S&P equivalent, last assessed Q4 2024).

## TechSupply Corp — Performance Review 2024

TechSupply Corp delivered 89.1% of orders on time in FY2024, falling below the 95% SLA threshold in Q2 and Q4. Root causes: component shortages for PCB assemblies (Q2) and a warehouse fire at the Pune facility (Q4). Recovery plan acknowledged and accepted by procurement; watch-list status assigned in November 2024.

Category coverage: IT Hardware, Electronic Components, Telecom Equipment. Average pricing benchmark delta: +4.2% above market (unfavorable). Two compliance audit findings raised in the last 24 months — one minor (documentation lapse) resolved; one moderate (export-control classification gap) remediation in progress as of Q1 2025.

## GlobalParts Ltd — Performance Review 2024

GlobalParts Ltd achieved 99.1% on-time delivery in FY2024 with zero SLA breaches. Average cycle time 3.1 days. Ranked #1 in category for Mechanical Components and Raw Materials. Won best-in-class supplier award from the procurement council in March 2025.

Pricing: 3.8% below market benchmark on average. Volume discounts available at 500+ unit thresholds. Last spot-check audit (October 2024): zero findings. ISO 9001:2015 certified. No geographic concentration risk — manufacturing distributed across Chennai, Hyderabad, and Coimbatore.

Financial health: $89M revenue, 11% YoY growth, zero outstanding legal disputes. Dependency risk: low — GlobalParts sources raw materials from 14 distinct tier-2 suppliers.

## FastTrack Logistics — Performance Review 2024

FastTrack Logistics handles last-mile delivery for the indirect procurement category. On-time delivery rate: 93.7%, slightly below the 95% SLA target. Primary failure modes: traffic congestion at metro delivery locations and manual consignment processing. Process improvement plan filed in Q4 2024.

One minor compliance finding: consignment tracking data not uploaded to the procurement portal within the required 2-hour window on 7 occasions. Remediation: automated upload script deployed December 2024, zero failures since.

Pricing: in-line with market benchmark (+0.8%). No financial distress signals. Operating in 14 cities with expansion to 4 more planned by Q3 2025.

## NovaTech Solutions — Performance Review 2024

NovaTech Solutions is a new supplier onboarded in Q3 2024 for IT Services and Software Licensing. Insufficient performance history for full reliability scoring (3 orders completed). Initial delivery: 100% on time. Pricing: 7.2% below market benchmark for software licensing.

Risk: single-location operations (Bengaluru). No ISO certification yet; application filed. One pending compliance certification (IS/IEC 27001 for data handling). Until certification is obtained, NovaTech is on conditional status for IT Services category with a value cap of $50,000 per order.
